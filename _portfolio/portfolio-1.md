## Featured Projects  

### 🏆 **DOCTOR SAM – AI Healthcare Chatbot**  
An AI-driven chatbot designed to **pre-screen patient symptoms and summarize medical history** for doctors.  
- **Winner**: AI Thailand Hackathon 2024  
- **Technologies**: Multi-Agent AI, ASR (Thai dialects), LLMs (Typoon 1.5x, OpenBioLLM)  
- **Impact**: Assists doctors in **small public hospitals** with limited staff  

### 🎤 **PathummaLLM – Thai Multimodal AI Model**  
Lead researcher in developing **NECTEC’s PathummaLLM**, supporting **Thai, Chinese, and English**.  
- **Key Features**: Multimodal processing (text, images, speech)  
- **Publication**: ACL 2024  

### 🏅 **Super AI Engineer SS4 – Image Captioning Winner**  
Achieved **1st place (Gold Medalist)** in an image captioning competition.  
- **Approach**: Transformer-based vision-language model

Research on **continual pretraining of LLMs** for Thai language understanding.  
- **Outcome**: Achieved results **comparable to GPT-4.0** in Thai NLP tasks
